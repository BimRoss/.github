# BimRoss

> We build software that prints proof, not promises.

Most companies optimize for noise.  
We optimize for outcomes: **increase revenue, decrease cost**.

## What We Build

- **Bittensor Subnet 42 operations**: validator + miner systems, infra, and tooling.
- **Production data products**: ingest, index, and automation pipelines around social and market signals.
- **Operator-first tooling**: GitOps, observability surfaces, and AI-ready interfaces that are easy for humans and agents to parse.

## How We Operate

- **Ship fast**: execution beats ideation.
- **Measure hard**: throughput, reliability, cost, and revenue decide what lives.
- **Automate relentlessly**: if it repeats, we systematize it.
- **Design for leverage**: one decision should pay off many times.

## Technical Spine

- **Backends**: Go
- **Frontends**: Next.js/React (SSR-first when content matters)
- **Infra**: Docker + Kubernetes + Rancher Fleet GitOps
- **Observability**: health surfaces + Grafana/Prometheus-oriented workflows

## Philosophy

You do not get paid for complexity.  
You get paid for solved problems.

If you want to collaborate, bring one of these:

1. A way to grow output without growing headcount.
2. A way to cut compute/network cost without killing performance.
3. A way to make operators faster and safer.
